# Title Partnership Agreement: Halcyon Energy and Meridian Racing

**Contract ref:** halcyon_v1 · **Currency:** USD · **Term:** one championship season

## 1. Parties
Halcyon Energy Inc. ("Sponsor") and Meridian Racing LLC ("Team").

## 2. Rights
Sponsor is the Title Partner of the Team for the season, with naming rights, primary livery placement and hospitality as set out in Schedule A.

## 3. Base fee
3.1 Sponsor shall pay a base fee of USD 24,000,000 for the season.
3.2 The base fee is invoiced in four equal quarterly instalments in advance on 1 January, 1 April, 1 July and 1 October, payable within 30 days of invoice.

## 4. Performance bonuses
4.1 USD 2,000,000 for each Grand Prix won by a Meridian car.
4.2 USD 750,000 for each Grand Prix podium finish by a Meridian car, other than a win.
4.3 Bonuses are determined by the final classification of the Grand Prix as published by the FIA after all appeals and penalty decisions. Sprint races do not count toward clause 4.
4.4 Team shall invoice earned bonuses after the result is final. Payment terms are 30 days.

## 5. General
5.1 Governed by the laws of England and Wales.
