# Official Timing Partner Agreement: Arcadia Watches and Meridian Racing

**Contract ref:** arcadia_v1 · **Currency:** GBP · **Term:** one championship season

## 1. Parties
Arcadia Watches Ltd ("Partner") and Meridian Racing LLC ("Team").

## 3. Base fee
3.1 Partner shall pay a base fee of GBP 5,000,000 for the season, invoiced quarterly in advance on 1 January, 1 April, 1 July and 1 October, payable within 30 days.

## 4. Performance bonus
4.1 For the purposes of this clause, "podium finish" means a final classified position of first, second or third.
4.2 GBP 400,000 for each podium finish by a Meridian car, including sprint races
4.3 Bonuses are determined by final results after all penalty decisions.

## 5. General
5.1 Governed by the laws of England and Wales.
