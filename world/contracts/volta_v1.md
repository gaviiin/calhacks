# Official Partner Agreement: Volta Telecom and Meridian Racing

**Contract ref:** volta_v1 · **Currency:** EUR · **Term:** one championship season

## 1. Parties
Volta Telecom S.A. ("Partner") and Meridian Racing LLC ("Team").

## 2. Base fee
2.1 Partner shall pay a base fee of EUR 8,000,000 for the season, invoiced quarterly in advance on 1 January, 1 April, 1 July and 1 October, payable within 30 days.

## 5. Performance bonus
5.1 EUR 100,000 for each points finish by a Meridian car
5.2 A points finish is a classified finish that scores championship points.
5.3 Bonuses are determined by final results after all penalty decisions.

## 6. General
6.1 Governed by the laws of the Netherlands.
