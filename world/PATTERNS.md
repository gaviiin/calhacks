# What is planted in the world

This is the checklist for the Policy Learner (2025 patterns) and the Cap Analyst (2026 plants). Everything here is for **seed 7** (`uv run python -m world.generate --seed 7 --out world/out`). Matchers use only fields a consumer sees (`account_id`, `vendor_id`, `department_id`, `memo`, and for bill-line patterns the bill line `description`), so a learned policy pattern can be compared with the row that planted it. The executable versions live in `world/patterns.py`; `world/tests/test_generate.py` fails if the counts below drift from the generated history.

## 2025 patterns (history_2025_*)

Counts: **precedents** are matched lines whose 2025 classification equals the 2025 treatment; **exceptions** are matched lines classified otherwise, each with a reason in `history_2025_cap_classification.reason`; **ERP corrected** counts matched lines where `erp_category_code` differs from the final classification (the ERP user was wrong and the analyst or the Head of Cost Cap fixed it). `RELEVANT+NON_F1` means two classification rows for the line (60% / 40%); `MARKETING+RELEVANT` (a mixed invoice) means one row per category, ids `h25c-NNNNN` and `h25c-NNNNN-2`, with the amounts in the bill-line classifications. The third table counts bill-line description patterns over `history_2025_bill_line` joined to `history_2025_bill_line_classification`: **precedents** are bill lines classified as the 2025 treatment, and **on multi-category bills** counts matched lines on an invoice whose lines carry more than one category.

### How to read the main ones

- **Canteen** (`ven-001` + 6410). RELEVANT in 2025 because Issue 22 has no catering exclusion. The expected 2026 policy is CATERING (D5.1.1.z) from 2026-01-01. This is the headline rule change, and the precedent is the wrong answer in 2026.
- **Wind tunnel offsite lunch** (`ven-001` + 6410 + "Northbrook Wind Tunnel"). RELEVANT in both years. Same vendor and account as the canteen, so a policy keyed only on vendor and account will misfire. The memo is the discriminator.
- **Competition travel**. Flights and hotels whose memo names a Grand Prix or pre-season test are COMPETITION_TRAVEL. The exceptions are driver travel (F1_DRIVER, 8) and Team Principal travel (EXCLUDED_PERSONS, 6). Other travel is RELEVANT; its exceptions are partner meetings (MARKETING), recruitment (HR_FINANCE_LEGAL) and Applied Technologies trips (NON_F1). The ERP user often codes non-race travel COMPETITION_TRAVEL, because that is the account default.
- **Race-weekend ground transport** (6330 + Grand Prix). RELEVANT in 2025 because ISS22 3.1(r) covers only flights, rail and hotels. COMPETITION_TRAVEL in 2026 because D5.1.1.r adds road transport. The ERP user coded 6 of these COMPETITION_TRAVEL in 2025 and was corrected.
- **Freight**. Its memos also name Grand Prix, but freight is RELEVANT: only personnel travel is excluded. Two large air freight lines (Japanese GP, Singapore GP) were ERP-coded COMPETITION_TRAVEL, followed by the analyst, and **rejected by the Head of Cost Cap** with that reason.
- **Kestrel Technology recharges** (`ven-003`, related party). 12 monthly invoices, each compared to `fv-001` (contract GBP 650,000, fair value GBP 620,000). Ten are invoiced at contract and stay RELEVANT. May and October carry a volume credit (GBP 520,000 < fair value), so they are **ADJ_RELATED_PARTY_FAIR_VALUE** and included at fair value. May was caught by the analyst; October was rejected by the Head of Cost Cap. The expected policy is "value at max(invoiced, fair value)".
- **Mixed Grove Catering invoices** (bill-line table). The gl memo and account say hospitality or canteen; only the bill lines say what was bought. Descriptions are the discriminator: "Sponsor dinner" / "Partner guest hospitality" lines are MARKETING in both years; "Factory canteen ..." lines (not for partner guests) are RELEVANT in 2025 and CATERING in 2026; "... lunch ... Northbrook Wind Tunnel" lines are RELEVANT in both years, even on a canteen or hospitality invoice. So the learnable 2025 split is MARKETING versus RELEVANT, and the 2026 truth for the same descriptions is MARKETING / CATERING / RELEVANT (P01, P43, P44).
- **Applied Technologies payroll** (dept-APPLIED, f1_activity_share 0.6). The ERP user codes the whole line NON_F1. The 2025 submission splits it into two classification rows: `h25c-NNNNN` RELEVANT (60%) and `h25c-NNNNN-nf1` NON_F1 (40%), which ISS22 allowed for any Non-F1 activity. **In 2026 the split is not permitted**: D5.1.1.h.c.A apportions Consideration only for staff also doing PU supply, Customer Team Support, FIA Projects or Standard Supply Components, and generic client work is none of these. Salaries, pension and staff expenses are 100% RELEVANT (P45); employer NI is SOCIAL_SECURITY. Utility and maintenance shares (D5.1.1.h.c.B, C) remain apportionable, but this world bills the campus utilities and maintenance wholly to F1. Client-project contractors and 6810 projects stay NON_F1 (Directly Attributable).
- **Bonuses** (5060). The 2025 March bonus round is a Formal Bonus Scheme paid on the team's points, podiums and race wins, which ISS22 counts as Employee Bonus Costs: BONUS. SECD-ISS07 narrows Employee Bonus Costs to a scheme determined solely by the final Constructors' classification and paid in full after the last Competition, so the 2026 annual performance, podium & race-win and retention bonuses are RELEVANT (P42) though the ERP codes them BONUS. The one genuine 2026 scheme is the BS-26 Constructors' Championship accrual (P07), which is BONUS up to the $12m Excluded Bonus Maximum and RELEVANT above it.
- **P&E depreciation** (7000). DEPRECIATION (excluded) in 2025 under ISS22 3.1(o). RELEVANT in 2026 (inside the cap, measured per D6.1.1.k). The one exception is the Applied Technologies battery lab, which is used only for non-F1 work and stays DEPRECIATION (D5.1.1.o.ii). Property depreciation (7010) stays DEPRECIATION.
- **Employer NI**. In 2025 the base (13.8%, account 5040) follows the department's treatment, and the excess over 13.8% (5041, from April) is SOCIAL_SECURITY (ISS22 3.1(q)). In 2026 all mandatory NI is SOCIAL_SECURITY (D5.1.1.q), except the Excluded Persons' NI, which is EXCLUDED_PERSONS.
- **Apprenticeship levy** (5110). Always RELEVANT. The ERP user coded Feb, Jun and Oct SOCIAL_SECURITY; all three were rejected on review (Red Bull 2021 item 6).
- **Sporting fines** (6910). RELEVANT in 2025, because ISS22 3.1(m) excludes only Financial Regulations penalties. The ERP coded all of them FINES and every one was corrected. They are FINES in 2026 (D5.1.1.m covers any FIA fine).
- **New 2026 exclusions** that were RELEVANT in 2025: fuel (6110, FUEL) and business continuity (6930, BCDR). Note that FUEL, CATERING, HEALTH_SAFETY and BCDR never appear in the 2025 history.
- **Health & safety** (6740). HR_FINANCE_LEGAL in 2025, because ISS22's Human Resources Activities include "health and safety matters". HEALTH_SAFETY in 2026 (D5.1.1.y), where SECD's HR definition drops them. The category changes even though both years exclude the cost.
- **Property** (6700 rent and rates). PROPERTY in both years. **Building maintenance** (6730, Cherwell Building Maintenance) is never Property Costs: RELEVANT in both years, though the ERP user codes every third invoice PROPERTY. **Buildings insurance** (6760) is RELEVANT in 2025 (ISS22 Property Costs are rent, lease, business rates and property taxes) and PROPERTY in 2026 (SECD adds property insurance).
- **Sick cover** (5200 + "sick cover"). SICK_COVER in 2025. The category does not exist in SECD-ISS07, so the right 2026 move is to ask, and the line is then RELEVANT.
- **RDEC credit** (7300, negative). RELEVANT in 2025: the credit reduces Relevant Costs under ISS22 4.1(b)(ii). ADJ_GRANT_GROSS_UP in 2026 (D6.1.1.b.i.A.1). The direction flips.
- **Escalation** (`history_2025_approval`). Every line with |amount_usd| >= $250,000 has `escalated_to = head_of_cost_cap`. Three smaller lines were escalated as the first of their kind (first sporting fine, first sick-cover claim, first disposal). `decided_by` on the classification is `head_of_cost_cap` exactly when the line was escalated. Rejections have `decision = rejected` and a reason naming the analyst's proposal and the correct category.

## 2026 plants (answer_key.csv)

45 plants, 127 rows. `expected_action` is one of `reclassify | ask | hold | deny | flag`. `world/out/truth_2026.csv` (not part of SCHEMA) gives the correct 2026 treatment of **every** gl_line, with the amount that counts toward Relevant Costs. Use it to score precision beyond the plants. `world/out/truth_2026_allocations.csv` (`gl_line_id, bill_line_id, basis, basis_ref, category_code, rulebook_entry_id, amount_usd_minor, plant_id, note`) gives the correct split of every 2026 line whose truth has more than one row: 10 mixed invoices split by `bill_line` (P01, P43, P44) and 2 lines at a category limit (P07 BONUS, P34 PU_SUPPLY), where the part within the limit has basis `cap_limit` and the Relevant excess `cap_limit_excess` (basis_ref = the limited category). Each line's allocations sum exactly to its `amount_usd_minor`; any rounding residue sits on the largest allocation.
